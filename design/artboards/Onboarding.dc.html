Welcome · 1 of 4 An experiment in creative world building. Use the cards to imagine how the world around you could turn out, ten years from now. One question per card. Answer the ones that spark something, set aside the rest.
Before you start · 2 of 4 Find a quiet spot. Put some music on. Close your eyes. Picture your world ten years out: the street, the Tuesday night, what counts as cool. We made a playlist for it.

How it works · 3 of 4 Tap a card to answer. Swipe it away to set it aside. Say it the way you would say it out loud; dictation works too. Set-aside cards come back another day. Please don't name real people.
